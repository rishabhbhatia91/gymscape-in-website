## Project Overview

Apex Sports Arena required converting an unused 8,500 sq.ft. outdoor open area into a state-of-the-art 3-court pickleball facility to host local tournaments and daily coaching academies in Lucknow.

The client required an international-standard cushioned acrylic surface that minimizes joint impact for senior players while delivering true, consistent ball bounce for competitive tournaments.

***

## Engineering & Execution Scope

Turnkey execution covering civil sub-base rectification, acrylic synthetic surfacing, tournament netting, perimeter enclosure, and anti-glare sports lighting.

### Key Milestones Delivered:

1. **Sub-Base Civil Surface Preparation:**
    * Precision diamond grinding of the concrete base to eliminate high spots and surface laitance.
    * Application of heavy-duty deep-penetrating epoxy primer for maximum chemical adhesion.
    * Application of acrylic resurfacer mixed with silica sand (Grade 80 mesh) to rectify low depressions and ensure a 1:120 water run-off slope.
2. **8-Layer Synthetic Cushioned Acrylic System:**
    * 2 coats of elastomeric rubber particle cushion coat for high shock absorption.
    * 2 coats of fine cushion acrylic formulation for uniform surface elasticity.
    * 2 coats of heavy-wear color concentrate (Tournament Navy Blue playing field with Forest Green safety runoff surround).
    * 2 coats of UV-resistant, textured polyurethane line markings in crisp white.
3. **Sports LED Floodlighting:**
    * 200W anti-glare sports floodlights delivering an average of 350+ Lux across all courts for night play.

***

## Results & Facility Impact

* Completed within **18 calendar days** from initial sub-base grinding to final tournament line marking.
* Successfully hosted its inaugural inter-club pickleball tournament with 60+ participants.
* Zero water pooling observed during heavy rains due to calibrated gradient engineering.
