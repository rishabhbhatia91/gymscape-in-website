When selecting outdoor playground equipment for schools, public parks, or gated residential societies, safety, durability, and weather resistance must take top priority. Sub-standard thin metal pipes or brittle plastics pose severe injury risks and rust away within months under the harsh Indian climate.

Here are the essential manufacturing standards and safety guidelines recommended by Gymscape's playground engineering team.

---

## 1. Material Specifications: What Makes Playground Equipment Safe?

### A. Galvanized Iron (GI) Structural Columns
* Standard cheap swings use Mild Steel (MS) black pipes that corrode rapidly from the inside out.
* **Gymscape Standard:** Heavy-gauge Grade-A Galvanized Iron (GI) structural class B/C pipes (minimum 3mm to 3.5mm wall thickness and 114mm outer diameter).
* **Surface Finish:** Multi-stage chemical de-greasing, phosphate treatment, and high-adhesion pure polyester outdoor powder coating (80–120 microns) resistant to UV peeling and salt-spray corrosion.

### B. Food-Grade UV-Stabilized LLDPE Rotomoulded Plastics
* Slides, spiral tunnels, roofs, and crawl tubes must be manufactured using **virgin Linear Low-Density Polyethylene (LLDPE)**.
* UV Class 8 stabilizers prevent discoloration, micro-cracking, and structural embrittlement under blistering 45°C summer heat.
* Free from sharp burrs, toxic lead stabilizers, or heavy metal pigments.

## 2. Safety Zones & Impact-Absorbing Surfacing

Every piece of playground equipment requires a designated **Safety Fall Zone** (minimum 6 feet / 1.8 meters radius clear of any obstacles, fences, or hard curbs):

* **Under Swings:** Clear distance of twice the height of the swing pivot point in both forward and backward directions.
* **Under Multiplay Slides:** Minimum 6 ft clearance at the slide run-out terminal with safe ground deceleration.
* **Safety Surfacing Options:**
  1. **EPDM Wetpour Seamless Rubber Flooring:** Premium 25mm to 36mm shock-absorbing cushioning with colorful custom graphics.
  2. **Interlocking Rubber Safety Pavers:** 25mm thickness high-density SBR tiles for modular replacement.
  3. **High-Density Artificial Landscape Grass with Shock Pad:** Soft, natural aesthetic with high impact protection.

## 3. Key Playground Station Checklist for Educational Institutions

1. **Toddler Multi-Play Station (Ages 2–6):** Low deck height (2 to 3 ft), gentle wave slides, sensory play panels, soft animal spring riders.
2. **Primary & Senior Multi-Play Station (Ages 6–14):** 4 to 6 ft deck height, spiral slides, rope suspension bridge, climbing net, loop climbers, and horizontal monkey bars.
3. **Heavy-Duty Swings & See-Saws:** Anti-wrap sealed ball bearing pivots, rubberized safety belt seats, enclosed tyre swings, and dual-axis dampening see-saws.
