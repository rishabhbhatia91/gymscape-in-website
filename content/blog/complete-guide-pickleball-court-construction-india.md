## 1. Official Pickleball Court Dimensions

According to international guidelines (USA Pickleball and IPF standards):

* **Court Playing Area:** 20 feet (6.10 m) wide × 44 feet (13.41 m) long.
* **Non-Volley Zone (The Kitchen):** 7 feet (2.13 m) extending from each side of the net.
* **Recommended Total Footprint:** 30 feet × 60 feet (1,800 sq.ft.) for recreational play, or 34 feet × 64 feet (2,176 sq.ft.) for tournament play with safety runoff margins.
* **Net Height:** 36 inches at sidelines and 34 inches at the center.

> **Pro Tip for Club Owners:** A standard tennis court area (approx. 60 × 120 ft) can easily fit **2 to 4 pickleball courts**, multiplying your player capacity and revenue per square foot.

## 2. Base Preparation: Concrete vs. Asphalt

The foundation is the most critical factor determining whether your court will last 10+ years or develop surface cracks within two monsoons.

1. **Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC):**
   * Grade M25 or M30 with a minimum thickness of 4 to 5 inches.
   * VDF (Vacuum Dewatered Flooring) / Tremix finish with a controlled 1:100 to 1:120 slope for efficient rainwater drainage.
   * Expansion joints cut and filled with polyurethane elastomeric sealant.
2. **Asphalt Sub-Base:**
   * Excellent flexibility and seamlessness, best for large multi-court outdoor arenas.

## 4. Perimeter Fencing & LED Floodlighting

* **Perimeter Fencing:** 10 to 12 ft high heavy-duty GI chainlink or powder-coated welded mesh with wind-break screening to prevent balls from flying out and ensure player safety.
* **Sports Lighting:** 4 to 8 pole sports-grade LED floodlights (150W – 300W) calibrated for 250–350 lux (recreation) or 500+ lux (tournament broadcast quality) with anti-glare diffusers.

## 5. What Does Pickleball Court Construction Cost in India?

A standard turnkey outdoor pickleball court (1,800 sq.ft.) in India typically ranges from **₹3.5 Lakhs to ₹8.5 Lakhs**, depending on:
1. Existing sub-base readiness (civil slab already cast vs. fresh excavation and RCC casting).
2. Number of acrylic cushion layers (5-layer standard vs. 8-layer cushion system).
3. Height and quality of fencing and LED lighting package.
