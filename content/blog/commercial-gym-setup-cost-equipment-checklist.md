Opening a commercial gym or setting up a high-end fitness centre in a residential township is a lucrative investment. However, improper space planning, incorrect equipment selection, or poor acoustic rubber flooring can quickly inflate costs and cause member dissatisfaction.

In this guide, the Gymscape fitness engineering team breaks down the essential equipment checklist, square footage requirements, and practical cost estimation for setting up a commercial gym in India.

---

## 1. Space Planning & Zone Distribution

For a 1,500 to 4,000 sq.ft. commercial gym, optimal member flow requires dividing the floor into 4 dedicated zones:

* **Cardio Zone (25–30% of floor space):** Positioned near front windows or high-ventilation zones for open visual appeal.
* **Pin-Loaded & Selectorized Machines (35%):** Safe, ergonomic guided machines for back, chest, shoulders, and legs.
* **Free Weights & Heavy Lifting Zone (25%):** Reinforced high-density rubber tile flooring, dumbbells, barbells, power racks, and adjustable benches.
* **Functional & Stretching Area (15%):** Kettlebells, battle ropes, plyo boxes, and yoga turf.

---

## 2. Essential Equipment Checklist

### A. Heavy-Duty Commercial Cardio
* **Motorized Commercial Treadmills:** 4.0 HP to 6.0 HP AC continuous-duty motors with auto-incline and shock-cushioned running decks (e.g. 4 to 6 units for a 2,000 sq.ft. gym).
* **Commercial Cross Trainers (Ellipticals):** Self-powered or electro-magnetic resistance.
* **Spinning Bikes & Air Bikes:** Heavy flywheel (18–22 kg) for high-intensity interval training.

### B. Selectorized & Plate-Loaded Strength Machines
* Dual Adjustable Pulley / Functional Trainer (8-stack or cable crossover).
* Lat Pulldown / Seated Cable Row combo.
* Chest Press / Incline Chest Press.
* Leg Press / Hack Squat 45-degree combo machine.
* Leg Extension & Seated Leg Curl.
* Multi-purpose Smith Machine & Half/Full Power Rack.

### C. Free Weights & Accessories
* Rubber Encased Hex Dumbbells / Polyurethane (PU) Dumbbells (2.5 kg to 40 kg pairs).
* Olympic Barbells (7 ft 20 kg, 5 ft, and EZ Curl bars with brass bushings).
* High-grade Olympic Rubber Bumper / Cast Iron Tri-Grip Plates (500 kg to 1,000 kg total).
* Heavy-duty Flat, Incline, and Decline (FID) adjustable benches (minimum 400 kg load rated).

## 3. Acoustic Gym Flooring: Why 20mm+ High-Density Rubber Tiles are Non-Negotiable

Standard tiles or concrete under free weights will crack within weeks. Gymscape supplies and installs:
* **High-Density EPDM / Recycled SBR Rubber Tiles (15mm to 25mm thickness):** Interlocking or bevelled edge with acoustic damping.
* **Impact Absorption:** Prevents floor damage from dropped 40 kg dumbbells while deadening vibrations for multi-storey buildings.
* **Non-Slip Grip:** Anti-microbial, sweat-resistant, and easy to sanitize.
