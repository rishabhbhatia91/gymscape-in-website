Opening a successful commercial fitness centre, corporate gym, or clubhouse fitness facility requires a well-calculated equipment mix. Buying redundant machines or omitting essential free-weight gear can lead to bottleneck queues during peak workout hours and member dissatisfaction.

In this definitive guide, the **Gymscape commercial setup team** shares the complete gym equipment checklist categorized by workout zones, along with recommended unit quantities based on facility size.

---

## 1. Commercial Cardio Zone Equipment Checklist

Cardio machines drive initial member engagement and warm-up routines. Commercial cardio units must feature heavy-duty frames and continuous-duty AC induction motors.

| Equipment Type | Recommended Specs | Units (1,500–2,500 sq.ft.) | Units (3,500–5,000+ sq.ft.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial AC Treadmills** | 4.0 HP – 6.0 HP AC Motor, 180 kg load, auto-incline | 4 to 6 Units | 8 to 14 Units |
| **Commercial Cross Trainers / Ellipticals** | Electromagnetic resistance, self-powered generator | 2 to 3 Units | 4 to 6 Units |
| **Spinning / Studio Bikes** | 18 kg – 22 kg flywheel, magnetic resistance brake | 2 to 4 Units | 6 to 10 Units |
| **Dual-Action Air Bikes** | Wind resistance fan, upper & lower body synergy | 1 to 2 Units | 3 to 4 Units |
| **Commercial Rowing Machine** | Air / magnetic resistance, foldable track | 1 Unit | 2 to 3 Units |

> [!TIP]
> *Learn more about commercial motor selection in our guide on [How to Choose a Treadmill: AC vs DC Motors](/blog/how-to-choose-a-treadmill-ac-vs-dc-motor-manual-guide/).*

## 2. Selectorized & Pin-Loaded Strength Machines

Selectorized guided machines allow safe, biomechanically correct resistance training for beginners and athletes alike without requiring a spotter.

### Upper Body Strength
* **Dual Adjustable Pulley / Cable Crossover (Functional Trainer):** The most versatile machine in any gym with dual 100 kg weight stacks.
* **Lat Pulldown & Seated Cable Row Combo:** Heavy-duty pulley for back width and thickness.
* **Chest Press / Incline Chest Press Machine:** Ergonomic converging arms.
* **Pec Fly / Rear Delt Combo:** Dual-function chest fly and posterior deltoid machine.
* **Shoulder Press Machine:** Guided overhead press.

### Lower Body Strength
* **45-Degree Leg Press / Hack Squat Combo:** Minimum 450 kg load capacity.
* **Leg Extension & Seated Leg Curl Machine:** Quadriceps and hamstring development.
* **Calf Raise Machine (Seated or Standing):** Isolated soleus and gastrocnemius loading.

### Core & Full-Body Guided Stations
* **Counter-Balanced Smith Machine:** Super-smooth linear bearings for safe squats and presses.
* **Assisted Chin-up & Dip Machine:** Counterweight assist for pull-ups and tricep dips.

## 3. Free Weights, Benches & Racks Checklist

The free weights area is the highest-traffic zone in any serious commercial gym.

### A. Dumbbells & Barbells
* **Rubber-Encased Hex Dumbbells / Polyurethane (PU) Pairs:**
  * 2.5 kg to 10 kg (Light pairs for warmups & lateral raises)
  * 12.5 kg to 30 kg (Core strength pairs)
  * 32.5 kg to 40 kg+ (Heavy progressive overload pairs)
  * Two 3-tier heavy steel dumbbell saddle racks.
* **Fixed Barbells (EZ Curl & Straight):** 10 kg to 30 kg with compact vertical tree rack.

### B. Olympic Bars & Weight Plates
* **Olympic Barbells:** 7 ft (20 kg, 700–1000 lbs rated with brass bushings) – 4 to 6 units.
* **EZ Curl Olympic Rods & 5 ft Rods:** 2 to 4 units.
* **Olympic Tri-Grip / Bumper Plates:**
  * Total 800 kg to 1,500 kg+ distributed across 2.5 kg, 5 kg, 10 kg, 15 kg, 20 kg, and 25 kg plates.
  * Olympic weight plate storage trees.

### C. Commercial Benches & Power Racks
* **Full Power Racks / Half Racks:** With safety spotter arms, J-hooks, and pull-up handles (2 to 3 units).
* **Adjustable Flat-Incline-Decline (FID) Benches:** 400 kg+ load-rated heavy-gauge steel (4 to 6 units).
* **Dedicated Flat Olympic Bench Press & Incline Olympic Bench:** With integrated spotter stands.
* **Preacher Curl Bench & Seated Military Utility Bench.**

## 4. High-Density Acoustic Rubber Flooring & Functional Gear

Standard concrete or commercial tiles will crack within weeks of dropping heavy dumbbells.
* **20mm High-Density Interlocking / Bevelled Rubber Tiles:** Shock-dampening EPDM/SBR tiles installed across the free weights and cardio zones.
* **Functional Accessories:** Kettlebells (4 kg to 24 kg), Battle Ropes, Plyometric Foam Boxes, Medicine Balls, and Resistance Bands.
