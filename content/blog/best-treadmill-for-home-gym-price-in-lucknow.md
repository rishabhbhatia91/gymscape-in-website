## 3. 4 Essential Home Gym Setup Tips for Treadmills

### 1. Acoustic Floor Matting is Essential
Never place a motorized treadmill directly on marble, vitrified tiles, or wooden flooring without protection. Motor vibrations and foot strikes travel through floors to rooms below.
* **Solution:** Place **15mm to 20mm high-density rubber gym flooring tiles** or a heavy EPDM shock mat beneath the treadmill feet.

### 2. Dedicated 15A Socket with Surge Protection
Motorized treadmills draw surge current during startup and motor acceleration. Plug the machine directly into a dedicated **15-Amp wall socket** and consider adding a 3kVA stabilizer if your area experiences voltage dips.

### 3. Space Planning: Safe Clearance
Allow at least:
* **2 feet (0.6 meters)** of clear space on either side of the treadmill.
* **4 to 6 feet (1.2 to 1.8 meters)** of open clearance behind the rear roller for safety in case of a trip or stumble.
